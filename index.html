Research

Nanoparticle membrane wrapping

Under the supervision of Prof. Mattia Bacca at UBC, I study how biomembranes deform as they adhere to and wrap around nanoparticles. Membrane bending, tension, and particle–membrane adhesion compete to determine whether wrapping proceeds, stalls, or reverses. Particle size and shape can change this balance.

My focus is on mathematical and numerical models of biomembrane elasticity, including wrapping around nonspherical particles. I am also interested in whether machine learning can help solve computationally demanding models, explore parameter spaces, and reveal interpretable relationships between membrane properties and wrapping behavior. These are research directions, not completed results, with potential relevance to nanoparticle uptake and drug delivery.
