Project Background

For UBC Engineering Physics ENPH 353, our team of two built an autonomous robot for a four-minute competition in Gazebo. It had to navigate roads, grass, and mountain terrain, avoid obstacles, read eight clueboards, and obey traffic rules.

High-Level Summary

The robot combined image-based navigation, obstacle detection, clueboard processing, CNN character recognition, and a modular ROS system. Camera data fed the controller and a separate clue detector, so sign processing could run alongside driving.

My contributions: I focused on clueboard detection, character trimming, and integrating recognition with the ROS system. My teammate implemented the PID driving algorithm, pedestrian detection, and the neural-network implementation. We jointly worked on roundabout navigation and training-data generation.

Clueboard Detection and Character Recognition

Our image pipeline used HSV color thresholds and contour filters to locate blue clueboards, removed the sky from the mask, corrected the board perspective, and separated its characters. A CNN then classified the cropped characters into 36 alphanumeric labels. Synthetic transformations varied blur, shape, and noise in the training images.

The final report records 100% training and validation accuracy on synthetic samples in the last epoch. That result describes the synthetic dataset; clue detection in the competition remained less reliable.

Navigation and Competition Outcome

For line following, the robot used thresholded camera images to find road boundaries and compare their centroids with the image center. The report describes terrain-specific masks for road, grass, and mountain sections, PID steering, and motion-based pedestrian detection.

Our team scored 29 points: 24 from the first four clueboards and a five-point tunnel bonus. The fifth board was missed in the grass section, disrupting later clue detection, and the robot did not reach the final board before the four-minute limit. The report recommends more reliable separation of connected characters and a fallback route to the tunnel.
